## CORRECTION — "PROVEN" is misnamed, and the number means the opposite of what it says

The `PROVEN` bucket is defined as *passes under the gate's construct, fails under the remedy*.
That predicate **aggregates two causes that point in opposite directions**, and labelling it
"the finding" named only one of them:

1. **A false green the remedy fixes** — the line passes today although its first command failed
   and should have sunk it.
2. **A correct line the remedy breaks** — the line passes today *because* it is deliberately
   testing a failure path, and the remedy's errexit kills it.

Reading the 19 members: they are almost entirely cause (2).
`out=$(python3 tools/validate-workflow.py tests/fixtures/invalid/E-XML-NODE-TYPE.xml 2>&1); echo "$out" | grep -q "E-XML-NODE-TYPE"`
is a *correct* test — the validator exits non-zero on an invalid fixture by design, and the
line asserts the right error code appears. Likewise `out=$(grep -c "…" file); test "$out" = "0"`:
`grep -c` exits 1 when it counts zero matches, which is exactly the condition being asserted.

**So the honest reading of this scan is:**

- **0 currently-manifesting false greens** were found in the real corpus. The defect is real and
  demonstrated (`tools/_t352-p011-errexit-probe.sh`, on a constructed fixture) but it is not
  presently firing anywhere in this tree.
- **4 latent instances** (the SUBSTRING-RISK class, all `grep -q "VALID"` against
  `validate-workflow.py`). These pass honestly today because their documents are valid. They
  become false greens the moment a document goes invalid — *precisely the case the check exists
  to catch*. Latency here is not safety; it is the check being untested in the only direction
  that matters.
- **19 lines the remedy would break**, of which the great majority are correct. This is the
  **blast radius**, not the finding.

This is the third instance on this arc of a difference spanning every cause while being reported
as one (T-343's `len(raw) − len(resolved)`; T-341's baseline). The predicate was right; the noun
