## Findings
- Search field: magnifier icon + placeholder "search catalog…" (empty, in the edit shots) / the typed query next to the icon. It sits under the
  "use a ref from this map" select in the value column of the ref row. It reads as a search box, not as a ref value; the ref value field above it stays separate
  (empty on v1; nid:attr-opportunity-id on style-A).
- Hits: each is its own bordered box in the value column. Notation is bright monospace on line 1 (it wraps at '.' for long ones such as
  sfdc:Opportunity.StageName / nid:attr-companyname-tacton-account). Label (source) · dataType is smaller and muted below it. Notation and label look
  clearly different. Nothing is clipped or truncated at 1024/1440/1920. Neighbouring hits are separated by their borders, and the boxes read as clickable choices.
- "3 more: type more words to narrow" sits small and muted under the last hit, directly above fieldRef (catalog-more-*-panel-end, catalog-more-1920).
- No match: "no match in the catalog" under the search field, then fieldRef follows (catalog-none-1024-panel).
- Belonging: the whole picker stays in the value column, after the ref label and before fieldRef, inside the yellow left rule of the open io editor. In every shot
  the attribute rows below (fieldRef, typeRef + its prefix warning, schemaRef, …) are pushed down, with no overlap.
- T-1302 vs T-1312 edit shots: the only structural difference is the extra "search catalog…" row under ref, which moves the rows below by one row. In v1-edit
  the panel scroll offset also differs (EXTENSIONS block higher). That is the scroll position, not a layout change.

## Minor (does not block)
- Label/dataType lines and the "N more" line use a small muted font (~9–10 px at these crops). It is readable but this is the smallest text in the panel.

Verdict: green.
